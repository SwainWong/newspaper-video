# PaperMotion API

Implementation: `assets/template/src/motion-paper.tsx`. Exported `PaperSpec` is a discriminated `{effect, data}` union; `PaperMotion` takes `{spec, frame, duration}`. `validatePaper(spec, duration)` returns runtime validation errors and the component rejects invalid input. `frame` is the effect-local frame, `duration` is the effect-local lifetime in frames (integer, minimum 30). The parent positions/scales the fixed 900 × 900 canvas inside its approved bounding box and supplies the local frame. Negative frames clamp to zero; frames after the lifetime clamp to its last frame. Every effect preserves its final state and reserves at least 15 frames of reading hold. Timing has no random, wall-clock, CSS transition or recurring pulse dependency.

`paper-fixtures.json` is an array of six `{effect,data}` specs, all valid for `duration:180`. Its SVG media is the original template's `public/template-example.svg`; the examples are technical fixtures, not production facts or a production narration.

| effect | data | behavior and constraints |
|---|---|---|
| chapter-tint | `color, opacity, at, duration` | Hex `#RRGGBB`; opacity 0–0.3; entry 9–18 frames. Parent puts this above paper texture and below all content, with evidence and skin in separate unaffected containers. No semantic color claim. |
| calendar-flip | `from, to, unit, start, end` | Distinct integer endpoints within ±999999; unit max 12 characters. Up to 12 monotonically sampled page transitions, each at least 8 frames; start/end bound the sequence. Each upper half rotates around the central seam before the next page's lower half lands. End holds exact `to`. Intermediate pages are expressly labeled as illustrative rather than observations. Only group start/end are appropriate paper sound anchors. |
| film-frame | `media:{type,src}, label, source, in, out` | `type` image/video; `src` public-relative local path; label/source max 52/100 characters. Fixed sprockets, 15-frame paper entrance starting at `in`, source always attached. `in/out` are effect-local playback bounds, not source trim frames. Muted `OffthreadVideo` starts at source frame zero and freezes at `out-in`; callers must supply a sufficiently long licensed clip. Source trim, if required, belongs in prepared media. Static images use `Img`. Holds at `out`; no fade out or additional pins/tape. |
| keyword | `subject, words:[{text,color,at,rotation}]` | Subject max 40 characters, 1–4 phrases, each max 24 characters; hex accent color; rotation ±6 degrees; anchors ordered at least 15 frames apart. Each complete phrase settles in 15 frames; no letter animation. Put fact phrases before emphasis and use narration word anchors. |
| icon-metaphor | `icon, meaning, caption, at` | Supplied local image, meaning max 40 characters, caption max 70. Explicit visible metaphor/non-evidence designation, 15-frame entrance; no invented icon sourcing or data encoding. |
| date-tiles | `date, format, at` | Valid ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`, format `zh` or `iso`; year/month/day tiles enter six frames apart and settle by `at+27`. Full unambiguous date remains visible. Calendar validity checked in UTC; no random digit rolling. |

All paths resolve through `staticFile`; URL schemes, absolute paths and traversal segments are rejected. The template supports local assets rather than unstable remote embeds. Validation checks schema, timing, text budgets and numeric bounds, but cannot certify rights, evidentiary truth, readability at an arbitrary parent scale or audiovisual acceptance. All six components are silent; parent motion planning owns explicit sound assets/rights/timing, bounding-box conflicts, narration anchors and real viewing/listening review. `icon-metaphor` and `date-tiles` motion remains a new design based on screenshot composition; implementation does not establish a recovered original trajectory.
